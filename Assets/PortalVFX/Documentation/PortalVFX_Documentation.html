1. Overview

Stylized Portal VFX is a library of 72 stylized animated portals with open, idle, teleport and close phases, plus 60 mix-and-match extras (ground runes, trails, frames, destination views), 40 sound effects and ready-to-use gameplay components: walk-through teleporting with a dissolve, linked portals with a live view, summoning, gravity wells, key-locked doors, wall and ceiling mounting, unstable portals and more.

  • 72 portal prefabs in 8 themes: Arcane, Dark, Elemental, Sci-Fi, Stylized, Classic, Signature and Utility.
  • Every portal lights its surroundings, has colour presets, quality levels, distance LOD and sound.
  • Custom lightweight shaders that render in Built-in, URP and HDRP with no shader swapping.
  • Portal Browser and Portal Constructor windows for placing, linking and building your own portals.
  • 9 demo scenes, including a playable showcase world and a live portal builder.

2. Quick start

  1. Open Tools > Portal VFX > Portal Browser.
  2. Pick a portal and press Place. It is dropped in front of the Scene view camera, facing it.
  3. Press Play. The portal opens by itself (Open On Enable on PVXPortal).
  4. To walk through it, select the portal and your character and use Self-link (step out of the same portal) or select two portals and use Link 2 portals. Then select the character and press Make teleportable.

Or drag any prefab from Assets/PortalVFX/Prefabs into the scene. A portal's front side faces its -Z (back of the blue arrow); its centre is about 2 m above the pivot.

3. Render pipelines

All shaders contain Built-in, URP and HDRP passes and are picked automatically. Nothing needs converting.

PipelineNotes
Built-inEnable Soft Particles in Quality settings for particles that fade where they touch geometry. Demo cameras use PVXBloom for glow; for your own camera use any bloom (Post Processing Stack v2 works).
URP 12+Enable Depth Texture on your URP asset for soft particles. Turn on Post Processing on the camera and add Bloom to a Volume for glow. Demo scenes do this automatically at runtime.
HDRP 12+Portals are unlit and emissive: use a Fixed exposure (EV 0 to 2) or they will be auto-exposed too dark or bright. Demo scenes set this automatically. Wet-floor planar reflections (demo only) are switched off; use an HDRP Planar Reflection Probe instead.
The demo scenes contain no pipeline-specific components. PVXDemoPostFX on the camera creates the right post-processing (PVXBloom on Built-in, a Volume on URP/HDRP) when you press Play.

4. Using a portal

Every prefab has a PVXPortal at its root. The quick settings at the top of its inspector change the whole portal at once:

SettingEffect
SizeUniform scale of the whole portal; particles follow.
SpeedSpeed of opening, closing, element motion and particles.
Tint / Hue ShiftRecolour every part, light included.
IntensityBrightness of every part, light included.
Open / Close / Teleport DurationTiming of each phase.
EventsonOpening, onOpened, onTeleport, onClosing, onClosed UnityEvents.

Call Open(), Close(), Toggle() and Teleport() (a flash for something passing through) from code or UnityEvents. State and OpenAmount tell you where it is.

Colour presets: PVXColorPresets holds the original look and three variants. Click one in the inspector or call Apply(index) / Next().

5. Teleporting

  • PVXPortalLink on a portal points at another link (or itself). Anything with PVXTeleportable that walks into it is pulled in, dissolves, and rebuilds in front of the other portal. The portal surface can show a live camera view of the other side.
  • PVXTeleportable on a character handles the dissolve (keeps the character's own textures via the PortalVFX/Dissolve shader), sparks and the pull-in. With Use Portal Color on, the dissolve matches the portal's colour.
  • Rigidbodies that fly into a linked portal come out of the other with matching speed (Pass Rigidbodies).
  • PVXScreenFX on the camera adds an FOV punch, shake and a swirl in the portal colour while the player passes.
  • Teleport from code: teleportable.TeleportTo(position, rotation, fromPortal, toPortal).

6. Gameplay helpers

ComponentWhat it does
PVXPortalTriggerOpens a portal without code: when a target comes near, after a delay, repeating, or manually; optional required key, auto-close timer, onLocked / onUnlocked events.
PVXKeyPickup / PVXKeyringA floating key the player collects; PVXKeyring.Add/Has/Remove from your own code.
PVXSummonSummon() raises a creature out of the portal, Dismiss() dissolves it away.
PVXGravityWellPulls nearby rigidbodies into an open portal; they spiral in and vanish (optional respawn).
PVXUnstableFlicker, brown-outs, shudder, size glitches and energy surges; Collapse() implodes and closes it.
PVXMountFloor, Wall or Ceiling: hides ground-only parts; PVXMount.Place() snaps a portal onto a surface.
PVXProximityThe portal glows brighter and its particles thicken as a target approaches.
PVXDistanceActivatorOpens portals as the player approaches and closes them when far, for big levels.

7. Editor tools

Portal Browser (Tools > Portal VFX > Portal Browser)

  • Search and filter all portals and extras; Place drops one in front of the Scene camera. With Place on surface on, it is mounted on the floor, wall or ceiling the Scene view is looking at (needs a collider).
  • Link 2 portals, Self-link, Make teleportable for the current selection.
  • Quality: apply Low / Medium / High to every portal in the scene.
  • Performance tab: particle systems, typical and maximum particle counts, materials and lights per portal; export as CSV.
  • Buttons that open each demo scene.

Portal Constructor (Tools > Portal VFX > Portal Constructor)

Build your own portal from layers: a base portal, a frame, a ground rune, up to two trails and a destination view. Recolour and resize it, press Preview in scene, then Save as prefab. Saved portals go to Assets/PortalVFX/Custom and appear in the Portal Browser.

8. Extras

Assets/PortalVFX/Extras holds 20 ground runes, 20 trails, 10 frames and 10 destination reveals. Each is a small portal in its own right (opens, idles, closes), so you can place it next to any portal or combine them in the Portal Constructor.

9. Performance

  • PVXQuality: Low (40 % particles, no distortion, no live view), Medium (70 %), High. Set one portal or all with PVXQuality.SetGlobal(level).
  • Distance LOD (on PVXQuality) thins particles and drops distortion as the camera moves away.
  • Fully closed portals hide their renderers and cost nothing to draw.
  • PVXPortalLight lights PortalVFX surfaces through shader globals (8 nearest portals), so it adds no real-time lights. The point light inside each portal can be disabled if you do not need it for your own materials.
  • The live view on linked portals renders an extra camera only while the viewer is within Live View Distance.

12. Scripting examples

using PortalVFX;
using UnityEngine;

public class OpenOnLever : MonoBehaviour
{
    public PVXPortal portal;

    public void Pull()
    {
        portal.onOpened.AddListener(() => Debug.Log("Portal is open"));
        portal.Open();
    }
}
// Link two portals at runtime and teleport the player.
var a = portalA.gameObject.AddComponent<PVXPortalLink>();
var b = portalB.gameObject.AddComponent<PVXPortalLink>();
a.other = b; b.other = a;
player.GetComponent<PVXTeleportable>().TeleportTo(target.position, target.rotation, portalA, portalB);
// Whole-game quality and a colour variant.
PVXQuality.SetGlobal(PVXQualityLevel.Medium);
portal.GetComponent<PVXColorPresets>().Apply(2);
portal.GetComponent<PVXUnstable>().Collapse();

14. Troubleshooting

  • Portals look flat or have no glow: add bloom (URP/HDRP Volume, Post Processing v2 or PVXBloom on Built-in) and use an HDR camera.
  • Particles show hard edges against the floor: enable Soft Particles (Built-in) or Depth Texture (URP).
  • Too dark or too bright in HDRP: use a Fixed exposure around EV 0-2.
  • Character does not teleport: it needs PVXTeleportable and a collider; the portal needs PVXPortalLink with Other set and must be open.
  • Pink materials: re-import the Shaders folder (right-click > Reimport).
